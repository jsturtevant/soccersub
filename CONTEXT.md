# Soccer game planning

## Language

**Team**: A coach-managed group of players with its own roster.

**Squad**: The players available for one game.
_Avoid_: Roster when describing game availability.

**Formation**: The arrangement of on-field positions, including one goalkeeper.

**Lineup**: The players assigned to the formation's positions at one time.

**Lineup block**: A planned lineup over one interval of game time.

**Plan template**: A reusable formation, timing, and lineup plan for a team.

**Game run**: One game played from an independent copy of a plan template.

**Half**: One of the two equal periods of game time.

**Halftime**: The break between halves; it does not count as playing time.

**Playing time**: The time a player spends on the field, including time as goalkeeper.
